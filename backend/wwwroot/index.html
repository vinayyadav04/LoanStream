Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is LoanStream a bank or a lender? ▾
No. LoanStream is a loan aggregator platform. We connect borrowers with RBI-registered NBFCs and banks. All lending decisions, approvals, and disbursals are made solely by the partnered lenders. LoanStream does not lend money directly.
Will checking eligibility affect my CIBIL score? ▾
No. Checking your eligibility on LoanStream is a soft inquiry and does not impact your CIBIL or credit score in any way. Only when a lender does a formal credit check (with your consent) will it appear on your credit report.
How long does it take to get the loan disbursed? ▾
Once your application is approved by the lending partner and KYC is complete, funds are typically disbursed within 24 hours directly to your bank account. Processing times may vary by lender.
What documents are required? ▾
Typically: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, last 3 months' bank statements, latest salary slip or ITR (for self-employed), and a selfie for KYC. All documents are submitted digitally — no physical copies needed.
